Paint guide

How to estimate ceiling paint separately from walls.

Ceilings use floor area as a starting point, but texture, stains, primer, and overhead application can change coverage.

Ceiling area is usually length times width

For a flat rectangular ceiling, area is room length multiplied by room width. A 12 ft by 10 ft room has a 120 sq ft ceiling. Multiply by coats and divide by paint coverage to estimate gallons. This is different from wall paint, where perimeter and wall height drive the area.

ConditionEstimate adjustmentWhy
Smooth ceilingUse label coverage.Closest to ideal coverage assumptions.

Water stainPlan stain-blocking primer.Paint alone may not block discoloration.
Dark or glossy old paintConsider primer.Improves coverage and adhesion.

Do not forget overhead supplies

Ceiling painting often needs extension poles, drop cloths, tape, roller covers, angled brushes, and eye protection. If the ceiling is high, stairwells or ladders may make the job more complex than the paint quantity suggests.

  • Calculate ceiling paint separately from wall paint.
  • Use flat ceiling paint unless the room needs special moisture resistance.
  • Prime stains before painting.
  • Protect floors, fixtures, and ceiling fans.
  • Plan extra time for overhead rolling and cleanup.
Estimator note: the paint calculator covers walls. For ceilings, calculate length x width as a separate paint line.
