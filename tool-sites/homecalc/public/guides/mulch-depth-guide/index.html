Landscape guide

How deep should mulch be?

Mulch depth changes both bag count and plant health. A refresh layer needs less material than a new bare-soil bed.

Depth is the main quantity driver

Mulch volume is bed area multiplied by depth. Doubling depth doubles the material. A 240 sq ft bed at 2 inches needs 40 cu ft of mulch. At 4 inches, the same bed needs 80 cu ft. Before buying, decide whether you are refreshing an existing bed or building coverage over bare soil.

DepthUse casePlanning note
1 inLight color refresh.Works only when old mulch remains.
2 inCommon maintenance layer.Good for many annual top-ups.

4 inBare soil or coarse mulch.Do not bury plants or block drainage.

Bagged or bulk mulch?

Bagged mulch is easier to transport, store, and spread in small areas. Bulk mulch is often more practical for large beds, but delivery fees and minimums matter. Use the mulch calculator to see cubic feet and cubic yards, then compare both buying methods.

Common mistakes

  • Piling mulch against tree trunks or plant stems.
  • Covering siding, weep holes, or foundation vents.
  • Ignoring existing mulch depth before adding more.
  • Measuring curved beds as one guessed rectangle.
  • Forgetting delivery and cleanup time.
Landscape rule: mulch should cover soil, not bury plants. Keep a clear ring around trunks and stems.
