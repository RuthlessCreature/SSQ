Privacy Policy

HomeCalc Tools runs calculator logic in the browser. The calculators do not require an account, and the site does not ask you to submit your name, address, phone number, or project photos to use the tools.

Measurements you type into the calculators are processed in your browser for the purpose of showing an estimate. The site is designed so ordinary calculator inputs do not need to be sent to a HomeCalc Tools application server.

Information you choose to send

If you contact the site by email, the message may include your email address, page URL, input values, screenshots, or other details you decide to include. That information is used to respond, troubleshoot, correct content, or evaluate requested improvements.

Analytics and ads

HomeCalc uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand visits and page performance. Its measurement script reports information about page loads, such as page path, referrer and performance timings. Cloudflare also processes web requests as our hosting and network provider.

HomeCalc currently has no active advertising placements. If Google AdSense is enabled, Google and its advertising partners may collect, use or share data from visits to serve, personalize or measure ads. Third parties may place or read browser cookies, or use web beacons, IP addresses, device identifiers and similar technologies. See how Google uses data on partner sites. Where applicable, privacy choices will be available when ads are enabled.

Result cards and sharing

Images and CSV files are generated in your browser. The purchase worksheet can also copy a link with your dimensions, prices and notes in the part after the #. That fragment is read by the browser and is not sent with a normal request for the page. Anyone you share the link with can read the inputs. Avoid entering private information in shared estimates. If you share a file or link with another service, that service handles it under its own policy.

Data retention

HomeCalc Tools does not maintain user accounts or saved project histories. Email records may be retained as needed for support, abuse prevention, legal compliance, or business records.
