How this drywall estimate is calculated

The calculator starts with wall area: room perimeter multiplied by wall height, minus the area of doors and windows. It then adds a waste allowance for cutoffs, mistakes, damaged corners, and layout decisions. The adjusted area is divided by sheet coverage and rounded up because drywall is sold as full panels.

Formula: wall area = 2 x (length + width) x height - openings. Area to buy = wall area x (1 + waste rate). Sheets = area to buy / sheet coverage, rounded up.
