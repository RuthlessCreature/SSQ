Measure an L-shaped floor in separate areas

Draw the finish boundary first, then add rectangles that share an edge without overlapping.

HomeCalc Tools | November 26, 2026

Choose one dividing line

Imagine a 12 ft by 10 ft main room and a 4 ft by 3 ft alcove projecting beyond its back wall. Measure the main rectangle to the alcove opening, then measure the alcove from that same line. The two areas are 120 and 12 sq ft. If both measurements start inside the same opening, the shared strip appears twice. If both stop short of it, it disappears.

A threshold 36 in wide and 4 in deep adds exactly 1 sq ft if the planned floor extends across it and neither adjacent area includes it. A fixed cabinet with a 2 ft by 3 ft footprint removes 6 sq ft only if the product will stop around the cabinet. The result is 120 + 12 + 1 - 6 = 127 sq ft. Check whether the flooring system allows the planned treatment around fixed cabinetry.

Convert inches by dividing by 12. Four inches is about 0.333333 ft. Preserve enough digits for the layout allowance and round the final purchase count up. The inches-to-feet conversion matters most near a package boundary.

Verify the drawing against the site

Measure both sides of a narrow hall. If it tapers, preserve its width at each end and use a shape appropriate to the actual geometry. For a straight-sided taper, the area is the run length multiplied by the mean of the perpendicular widths. Keep the taper on the sketch so it stays visible when reviewing cuts. For bowed or angled walls, record the offsets and ask the installer to check the layout where exact dimensions matter.

At a 10% allowance, 127 sq ft becomes 139.7 sq ft. Seven nominal 20 sq ft cartons give 140 sq ft, a margin of 0.3. Forgetting a 1 sq ft threshold changes the target by 1.1 sq ft. In a job this close to the boundary, take the drawing back to the room and confirm every doorway and whether the selected product really labels the box 20 sq ft.

Pass the sketch forward

Put a finish name on each rectangle and mark the transition between finishes. A later product change should affect carton yield and price without quietly changing the room area. Enter the areas as separate lines in the purchase worksheet and keep the dimensioned sketch with the export.
