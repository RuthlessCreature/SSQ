Reconcile delivered cartons with the estimate

Reconcile the labels and units that arrived before relying on a saved takeoff.

HomeCalc Tools | November 26, 2026

Compare the physical delivery with the quote

Keep the purchase order and the estimate side by side. Verify product identifiers, the package count, carton coverage and any lot information used by the manufacturer. Check whether the shipment is complete or has a split-delivery line. Photograph discrepancies for your own records and contact the supplier promptly under the order terms.

Suppose the order was five cartons at 15.49 sq ft and the target was 62.30 sq ft. The delivery sheet lists five, but only four matching cartons can be located at the job. The usable cartons represent 61.96 sq ft. The immediate planning shortage is 0.34 sq ft, yet the smallest reliable replacement may be a whole matching carton.

A box with a different label may be a replacement, a separate item or a mismatch. Verify the reason and suitability with the supplier. An alternate format can shift both layout and carton yield. Use the actual coverage and plan for that format before accepting it, and keep replacement stock separate until the issue is resolved.

When a carton is damaged, record how many sound pieces are actually usable and whether any need to be saved for claims. Deduct the unusable quantity only after inspection. A package whose seal is damaged may still contain usable material, but a count can remain disputed until the supplier responds.
