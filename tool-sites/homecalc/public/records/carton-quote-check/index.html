Compare carton quotes at checkout

Use a supplier's complete carton quote to compare delivered cost for exactly the same room.

HomeCalc Tools | November 26, 2026

Start with an order quantity, then price it

A price per square foot is a rate. The amount charged depends on whole cartons, which can disagree even when two products are quoted for the same area. Record the label coverage and the supplier's actual carton price. If the quote only shows a rate, ask the supplier to confirm the extended carton price after rounding money and applying any discounts.

For a 124 sq ft target, product A covers 19.5 sq ft per box and costs $48.50. It needs seven boxes, so the material is $339.50. Product B covers 21 sq ft per box and costs $55. It needs six boxes, or $330. Both cover the job, but A purchases 136.5 sq ft versus B's 126 sq ft. The extra stock may be useful or may be charged and wasted. Write the conditions beside each price

Ask for the style and color code, label coverage, number of unopened cartons available together, expected arrival date, and whether collection or delivery is included. Record whether an unopened box may be returned, the deadline, and who pays freight. Keep the quote date because a later order can come from a different batch or price list.

If A lets you return unopened cartons, the seventh carton is still needed for the estimate. Six boxes provide only 117 sq ft, seven square feet short of the target. A possible future return has zero value in your initial order budget. Record it as a separate scenario after installation, once you know which units remained sealed.

Compare required accessories and product suitability separately. A low carton total can hide a needed transition profile or an accessory that is unavailable from the supplier. Bring the product's identifier and room dimensions to the installer or counter so changes can be traced to one line.

Keep the quote with the calculation

Download the dimensions, allowance, purchased area and material subtotal from the purchase worksheet. Add delivery, tax, extras and return terms to the supplier quote before choosing. The bathroom comparison shows how carton coverage alone can change the order.
