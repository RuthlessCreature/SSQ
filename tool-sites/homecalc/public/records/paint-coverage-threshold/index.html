Use the paint label to bracket a purchase

Calculate the package count at both ends of the manufacturer's coverage range before buying.

HomeCalc Tools | November 26, 2026

Record the surface and each product

On a room sketch, mark wall faces receiving the same product, color and sheen. Record their total square footage after any openings you plan to exclude. Label the ceiling, accent wall, trim and primer as separate lines when they use distinct products. Adding unlike coatings into one gallon total conceals that each gets bought independently.

Use the coverage range for the exact product and base on the label or technical sheet. For example, Behr Premium Plus Interior Eggshell No. 2050 lists 250 to 400 square feet per gallon. The published range describes the product on suitable surfaces. Your chosen coverage is still a planning input. Have the supplier or installer check an unusually porous, textured, repaired or untested surface.

Work backward from the container

Suppose 330 sq ft needs two finish coats. The target is 660 sq ft. At 400 sq ft per gallon it takes 1.65 gallons. At 250 it takes 2.64. With one-gallon packaging the range is two to three cans. For two cans to cover the job, the break-even coverage is 330 sq ft per gallon. If you plan to keep 0.25 gallon for repairs, the work can consume at most 1.75 gallons, so the threshold rises to about 377.14 sq ft per gallon.

One-gallon package count for the sample room
DecisionThreshold for two cans
Two coats, no reserved volume660 / 2 = 330 sq ft/gal
Two coats, 0.25 gal retained660 / 1.75 = 377.14 sq ft/gal
Three coats, no retained reserve990 / 2 = 495 sq ft/gal

The third coat moves the threshold beyond the selected label range. This makes the coat decision more valuable than refining minor opening deductions. Before buying, settle coat count and the reserve, then recheck the package comparison.

Keep a record for the next run

Save the product identifier, base, sheen and color, the coverage range and chosen value, paintable area, coat count, and available size. A documented partial can can be subtracted from needed volume when it is compatible and the usable quantity is known. Treat unknown leftovers as zero until verified. The paint calculator accepts measured wall area. The bedroom record shows how to group individual faces before entering it.
